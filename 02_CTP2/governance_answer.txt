What was missing at ShopFast was the contract: nobody had agreed and enforced, as code, which unit "amount" must be in (euros, not cents) and what range is plausible, so the payment team's silent switch to cents produced values that were perfectly valid (not null, unique, right currency) and still 100x wrong — only a distribution/range test (mean, max, decimal format), not an owner's sign-off after the fact, would have caught it before it shipped.
